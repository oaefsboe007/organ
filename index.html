Virtual Pipe Organ

5 manuals + 32-note pedalboard, ~210 stops, 25 couplers, octave couplers, tremulants, swell shoes, crescendo, sforzando, sustain, pistons, temperaments, MIDI. Click stops/keys, or type: lower row Z S X D… = pedals, upper row Q 2 W 3… = active manual. ↑↓ octave · ←→ swell shoe · PgUp/PgDn manual · Space sustain · Esc panic. Synth approximations; speakers can't reproduce 32′/64′ fundamentals. Stop-knob colours: gold principal · blue flute · green string · red reed · teal percussion. Violet = drawn by crescendo/sforzando.
Pedal — AGO 32 notes C2–G4 (MIDI ch1, notes 36–67)

About this virtual pipe organ

A free online pipe organ simulator that runs in your browser, with no download or sign-up. Play five manuals (Choir, Great, Swell, Solo and Echo) and a 32-note AGO pedalboard using the mouse, touch screen, computer keyboard or a MIDI keyboard or pedalboard. Draw more than 200 stops modelled on famous organs: Haarlem St Bavo, Hamburg St Jacobi, Paris St-Sulpice and Notre-Dame, Liverpool Cathedral, Sydney Town Hall and Atlantic City.

Features: 25 couplers, sub and super octave couplers, tremulants, expressive swell shoes, a 10-step crescendo pedal, sforzando, general and divisional pistons you can set yourself, adjustable cathedral reverb, historic temperaments (Werckmeister, Kirnberger, Valotti, meantone, Pythagorean) and Web MIDI input. Works best in Chrome or Edge; sound works in Firefox and Safari too.
